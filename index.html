Mohafiz Studio

The private publishing tool of the Watan Ke Mohafiz channel.

Watan Ke Mohafiz makes researched, documentary-style videos about military history, equipment and the lives of people in uniform, for YouTube and Instagram.

Mohafiz Studio is the software the channel owner uses to prepare and publish those videos. It is not a public service: it is used only by the channel owner, only for the channel owner's own accounts.

What it does

  • Prepares videos from public-domain and openly licensed sources, with credits.
  • Uploads the finished videos, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account.
  • Helps the owner read and reply to comments on the owner's own videos.

It uses the YouTube API Services. By using anything published with it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Road to Kakul — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Road to Kakul is an independent preparation app for candidates applying to the Pakistan Army, Navy and Air Force. It is not affiliated with any armed force, ISSB or academy.

What stays on your phone

Your name, chosen course, practice results, PT logs, written answers and progress are stored only on your device. We do not run a server that collects them, and you need no account.

Payments

Premium is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see your card or bank details. Google tells the app only whether you own Premium.

Ads

Free users can choose to watch rewarded ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads, as described in Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a notification on your phone. No data is sent anywhere.

Content updates

When online, the app downloads a public file of news and practice questions from GitHub. This request contains no personal information.

Children

The app is meant for candidates aged 16 and over and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.
